Privacy policy

This policy covers the current Weight Freak preview.

App data

The released preview stores appearance preferences on your device. It contains no advertising or analytics.

Verification builds

Optional Google authorization checks use an account you choose. Import requests account-wide read-only Drive access; output requests access to app-authorized files. These checks access no files and discard returned credentials. Google may retain your consent until you revoke it.

Development builds can import daily food and energy records from Drive folders you confirm. Google grants account-wide read-only access; folder restrictions are enforced by the app. Imported records and connection choices stay on your device. Disconnecting retains imported history and does not revoke other connections.

Development builds can read weight history from Health Connect with your permission. Selected-source measurements are stored on your device for daily summaries. Optional history and background permissions extend these reads. The app does not modify Health Connect records. Disconnecting retains imported history.

Development builds can use Google Picker to confirm an existing output folder with a separately chosen account. Setup reads folder metadata only, stores connection choices on your device and creates no files. Selecting a folder does not grant access to every existing child.

Optional encrypted backups can include imported health history and app settings. When you request a backup or enable daily backups, the app uploads encrypted files to your chosen Drive destination. The recovery key stays on your device unless you save it elsewhere yourself; credentials are excluded from backups. Daily backups are off by default.

Storage and deletion

Local app data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it through Android settings. The app does not use Android cloud backup.

This website

These pages contain no forms, cookies, analytics, scripts, or external assets. The hosting provider may process connection information to serve them.

Future features

This policy will be updated before integrations that access or share additional data become available. Those features will require the relevant permissions.
